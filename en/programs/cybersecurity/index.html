AI inside the plan

Four courses in the study plan bring artificial intelligence and cybersecurity together, across levels two, three and four.

  1. Level 2 Artificial Intelligence Intelligent agents, search, game playing, knowledge representation and logic, reasoning under uncertainty, planning and introductory learning methods. AI 106
  2. Level 3 Pattern Recognition for Cyber Security The role of AI and machine learning in security: automation, malicious bots, protecting web applications, case studies and managed security service providers. SC 301
  3. Level 3 AI Security Issues Cybersecurity fundamentals, AI's effect on security, trapdoors and malicious attacks against AI systems, and smart attacks that use AI. SC 303
  4. Level 4 Machine Learning Feature representation, dimensionality reduction, supervised and unsupervised learning, evaluation, neural-network basics and reinforcement learning. AI 203
