Level 2

Systems, networks and the first AI course

Operating systems, data structures and algorithms, databases, computer networks, cloud computing and artificial intelligence, then the first summer of field training.

AI courses

First semester

Fall

17 credit hours
  • 3 CH

    Operating Systems

    OS structure, processes and threads, synchronisation, scheduling, memory and virtual memory, I/O, file systems, virtual machines, and security and protection.

    2 lecture + 2 practical hours a week Requires: Programming (2) CS 203
  • 3 CH

    Advanced Calculus

    Multivariable functions and partial differentiation, first-order differential equations, Laplace and Fourier transforms and their applications.

    2 lecture + 2 practical hours a week Requires: Calculus BS 201
  • 3 CH

    Logic Design

    Digital logic and Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential circuits, flip-flops and counters, and synchronous and asynchronous design.

    2 lecture + 2 practical hours a week Requires: Physics CS 105
  • 3 CH

    Data Structures & Algorithms

    Lists, stacks, queues, trees and search trees, heaps, tries, hashing, sorting and searching, and basic graph algorithms.

    2 lecture + 2 practical hours a week Requires: Programming (1) CS 201

Second semester

Spring

17 credit hours
  • 3 CH AI

    Artificial Intelligence

    Intelligent agents, search, game playing, knowledge representation and logic, reasoning under uncertainty, planning and introductory learning methods.

    2 lecture + 2 practical hours a week Requires: Discrete Structure AI 106
  • 3 CH

    Advanced Statistics & Probability

    Normal-based distributions (chi-squared, t, F), estimation and confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, line fitting, ANOVA, stochastic models and Poisson processes.

    2 lecture + 2 practical hours a week Requires: Statistics & Probability BS 207
  • 3 CH

    Cloud Computing

    Cloud platforms and infrastructure, parallel programming in the cloud, distributed storage, virtualisation, cloud security and performance.

    2 lecture + 2 practical hours a week Requires: Operating Systems CS 206
  • 3 CH

    Introduction to Database

    Data modelling, the relational model and algebra, normalisation, query languages and optimisation, indexing, transactions, concurrency and recovery.

    2 lecture + 2 practical hours a week Requires: Programming (2) CS 202
  • 3 CH

    Computer Networks

    Network architecture and layering, application-layer protocols, socket programming, the transport layer (TCP, reliable delivery, congestion and flow control) and network-layer addressing and routing.

    2 lecture + 2 practical hours a week Requires: Operating Systems CS 205
